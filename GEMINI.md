# Product North Star: Modern, Efficient, Sleek UI/UX & Motion

Keel's core design ethos is: **"A modern app that is efficient and has sleek UI/UX and motions."**

---

## Guiding Principles

### 1. Lead Design Engineer Mindset
When working on any UI/UX or motion-related task, operate with full creative ownership rather than bare-minimum patch fixing. Proactively craft complete micro-interactions, tactile press feedback, and fluid transitions that feel refined and intentional.

### 2. Always Leverage Available Motion & Design Skills
For any UI, UX, styling, or animation task, always actively reference and apply the design principles from the available skills (such as `emil-design-eng`, `apple-design`, and `animate`):
- **Tactile Feedback**: Interactive surfaces should feel physical and responsive under the cursor rather than numb.
- **Continuous Motion**: State changes with spatial meaning (chevrons, toggles, popovers, drawers) should glide naturally with spatial continuity instead of snapping.
- **Natural Entrances & Exits**: Floating surfaces and popovers should emerge smoothly from their trigger origin.
- **Concentric & Clean Geometry**: Maintain harmonious corner curvature, comfortable breathing room between interactive elements, and clear hierarchy between active and hover states.
- **Efficiency First**: Motion must clarify and delight without feeling sluggish or impeding user workflows.

### 3. Pre-Completion Reflection
Before concluding a UI task, inspect the visual result (using available screenshot tools or live views) and ensure:
- Does this feel fast, tactile, and sleek under the cursor?
- Does every hover, click, and transition feel like polished, modern software?
